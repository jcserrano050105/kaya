How It Works

From “may sira” to fixed — in five steps

KAYA keeps you in control at every stage: you know who’s coming, what it costs, and where the job stands.

  1. 1

    Describe your household problem

    Type it the way you’d say it to a neighbor — in English, Filipino or Taglish. Mention more than one problem if you have them.

    You type
    “Tumutulo ang gripo at sira ang switch ng ilaw.”
  2. 2

    Receive recommended services

    KAYA matches your words to the right service categories. Several problems? We’ll suggest an All-in-One worker so it’s one visit.

    • Electrical – Switch Replacement

    Choose a verified worker

    Compare Trust Profiles: verified identity, verified skills, years of experience, completed jobs, ratings and availability for your schedule.

    Approve the estimate

    Your worker inspects the job and sends an itemized estimate — labor, materials and service fee. They cannot start until you approve it, and you can ask for a revision.

    Track and complete the service

    Follow each status live — accepted, on the way, arrived, in progress, completed. Pay by Cash, GCash or QR, get a recorded reference, then rate your worker. Repairs include a 30-day warranty.
